Fix and improve the email notification system for the ecommerce store.

Current issues:
1. Order confirmation emails are delayed (sometimes 2–10 minutes).
2. Some orders do not trigger the confirmation email at all.
3. Email design is basic and does not match the store theme.
4. No abandoned cart email system exists.

Use the following SMTP environment variables:

SMTP_USER="zayelle.in@gmail.com"
SMTP_PORT="587"
SMTP_PASS="YOUR_APP_PASSWORD"
SMTP_HOST="smtp.gmail.com"
SMTP_FROM="Zayelle <zayelle.in@gmail.com>"
EMAIL_PROVIDER="smtp"

--------------------------------
PART 1 — FIX ORDER CONFIRMATION EMAIL
--------------------------------

Requirements:

1. Send the confirmation email immediately after an order is successfully placed.
2. Email must trigger directly in the order creation function.
3. Do not delay email sending with background queues unless retry is needed.
4. If sending fails, retry once and log the error.
5. Add logs:
   - "Order created"
   - "Sending confirmation email"
   - "Email sent successfully"

PART 4 — ADD ABANDONED CART EMAIL SYSTEM
--------------------------------

Automatically send abandoned cart emails.

Logic:

1. If a user adds products to cart but does not checkout for 30 minutes:
   send abandoned cart email.

2. If still no purchase after 12 hours:
   send reminder email.

3. If still no purchase after 24 hours:
   send final reminder.
